01 / About

Optimization for complex supply chain decisions.

Hello, I am Yong Jin Lee, a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial Management Engineering at Korea University.

My research focuses on decision-making problems in supply chains, particularly vehicle routing problems, location-inventory-routing problems, and approximation algorithms.

02 / Research

Research focus

01

Humanitarian Logistics

I address diverse disaster scenarios using location-inventory-routing, location-routing, task-allocation, and related optimization methods.

02

Logistics Decision-Making

I study reverse logistics and closed-loop supply chains using vehicle routing, inventory routing, and integrated optimization methods.

03

Technology Intelligence

I investigate emerging technologies through patent analytics, technology roadmapping, and data-driven decision-support methods.
